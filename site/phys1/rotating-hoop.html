Lengths are in hoop radii r; 𝜏 = t√(g/r). The equation is d²β/d𝜏² = sin β (Ω² cos β − 1). The hoop's rotation is φ = Ω𝜏. In the rotating frame the energy E = ½(dβ/d𝜏)² + U(β) is conserved, with U(β) = −cos β − ½Ω² sin²β in units of mgr, shifted by a constant so its minimum is zero. At Ω > 1, the two stable angles are ±arccos(1/Ω²).

Force vectors in the rotating frame show gravity, centrifugal force, and the in-plane normal force in units of mg. The Coriolis force is perpendicular to the hoop's plane.
