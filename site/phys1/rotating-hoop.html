Radius is normalized to r = 1. Physical time is t; the simulation uses dimensionless time τ = t√(g/r). It solves d²θ/dτ² = sinθ(Ω² cosθ − 1) with θ(τ = 0) = β and dθ/dτ = 0 initially. The hoop turns through φ(τ) = Ωτ. No friction is added.
